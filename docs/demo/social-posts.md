## LinkedIn

What happens when you take away the evidence behind an AI answer?

I built EvidenceDoc to make that question testable.

Using NVIDIA Nemotron, it discovers entities and useful fields in documents, links supported values to visible source regions, and lets you click to zoom into the evidence. An interactive citation graph helps you explore repeated values across pages, while document comparison shows what changed between reports.

My favorite part is Evidence Lab: withhold the cited regions in a separate copy, then ask the model again without giving it the previous answer.

In the recorded demo, a dividend date was supported by the original report. After its source regions were withheld, a fresh NVIDIA model call returned no supported answer. You can inspect and export the experiment.

The demo uses original NVIDIA earnings reports and a scanned World Bank agreement, with actual Nemotron Super and Nemotron Omni calls.

Open-source code, real-document results and English demo videos:
https://github.com/ahmetcankaraoglan/evidencedoc

Built for the NVIDIA GTC Golden Ticket challenge. Thank you, Merve Noyan, for sharing it!
